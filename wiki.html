Vision, priorities and development status for Axiom Engine.

Axiom Engine is an experimental Roblox Studio development project focused on AI-assisted 3D modeling, scene construction, and programming with efficient credit usage.

Development status: This wiki describes the project's vision and planned capabilities. It does not claim that these features are already implemented or that a specific reduction in credit usage has been achieved.

Our mission

Make sophisticated Roblox development more accessible by giving AI agents efficient tools for modeling, building, and programming—without requiring excessive model calls or wasted credits.

Complex 3D Modeling

Enable AI to construct detailed, structured models and environments using precise spatial operations.

Building & Programming

Support scene construction, object editing, Lua scripting, and interface development inside Roblox Studio.

Credit Efficiency

Reduce repeated requests by reusing project context, coordinating tasks, and preferring efficient tool operations.

How we plan to approach it

  • Shared project context: retain useful information about the Roblox project to avoid repeated discovery.
  • Structured building tools: perform precise changes through reusable operations rather than lengthy manual instructions.
  • Agent coordination: explore collaboration between Google Antigravity and OpenAI Codex while reducing conflicting edits.
  • Validation and iteration: inspect results and make targeted corrections rather than rebuilding everything.

Roblox Studio MCP connection

AI-assisted workflows depend on activating and configuring the Roblox Studio MCP Server and connecting a supported client. See the setup documentation for illustrated instructions and optional plan recommendations.

Current Priority: Blender → Roblox Studio

Our current development focus is enabling AI agents to model complex objects in Blender, bring them into Roblox Studio, locate the intended destination, and place them with appropriate scale, orientation, and alignment. We are investigating reliable import workflows and ways to reduce unnecessary AI credit usage. These capabilities are goals under development, not completed features.

Development Schedule & Updates

Axiom Engine is an independent project maintained alongside work, school, and other personal responsibilities. As a result, development time is limited and updates may be irregular. There is no fixed release schedule or guarantee of frequent updates.

Thank you for your patience and interest. I will continue improving the project whenever time allows, prioritizing stability, security, and meaningful progress over rushed releases.
